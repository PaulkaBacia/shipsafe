How ShipSafe works

  1. A buyer pays through PayPal checkout.
  2. ShipSafe reads the PayPal payment (Seller Protection status, payer country, ship-to address) and runs 11 fraud checks.
  3. AI reviews the facts and the buyer's note, and explains the risk in plain words.
  4. Risky orders are put on hold automatically. You can ship, keep holding, or refund through PayPal in one click.
Safety floor: the AI can raise a risk score but can only lower the rule score by 15 points, so a buyer can't talk it into "safe."
